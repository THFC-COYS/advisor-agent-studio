Set the approval matrix, then name the owner

This is the governance step, and for an advisor it is the whole product. Your agent works close to client trust and client data, so this edition is the strictest in the Studio line: nothing here defaults to Runs freely. Pure drafting may notify you after the fact; everything else asks first. This practice runs on review.

Studio rule: no owner, no agent. An advisor's agent without a named accountable licensed advisor does not ship. The final package will not generate until the owner is named, and the package states plainly that the agent's output is preparatory and the advisor is responsible for all advice and client communications.
